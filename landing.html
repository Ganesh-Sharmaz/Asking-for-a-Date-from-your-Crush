Good questions

Before you press send.

Does the recipient need an account?

No. They only need the link you share. Your creator dashboard is separate from the recipient experience.

Can I edit or delete my link?

Yes. Open the builder page on the same browser where you created it to manage your saved links.

How many free links can I create?

The free experience supports two links per creator. Paid plans can be added later for more links.
